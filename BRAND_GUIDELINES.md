## 1. Executive Summary & Brand Strategy

### 1.1 Brand Identity & Proposition
**The Best Home Baker** is an elevated bespoke cake atelier and private event pâtisserie. Founded on unhurried culinary craftsmanship, the studio creates sculptural, architectural cakes for weddings, milestone celebrations, and intimate gatherings.

The name **The Best Home Baker** celebrates the warmth, care, and bespoke attention of an artisanal home hearth, elevated by Michelin-caliber pastry techniques, premium European pantry staples, and edible botanical design.

### 1.2 Target Audience & Core Client Profiles
- **Couples & Wedding Planners:** Seeking statement multi-tiered cakes with organic deckled edges, pressed edible florals, and custom flavor flights that harmonize with modern table design.
- **Milestone Celebration Hosts:** Parents and hosts organizing 1st, 21st, 30th, 50th, and 80th birthdays where mass-produced store cakes do not meet the aesthetic standard of the event.
- **Corporate & Gallery Hosts:** Demanding punctual, white-glove climate-controlled delivery, impeccable hygiene certifications, and bespoke dessert tables for brand activations and galas.

### 1.3 Core Metaphor & Brand Promise
- **The Core Metaphor:** *Sculptural Gastronomy & Edible Botany*. We treat every cake as both an architectural sculpture and a living garden canvas.
- **The Brand Promise:** Freshly baked from scratch within 36 hours of celebration pickup. Zero premixes, zero artificial shortening, zero synthetic extracts.
- **Tagline:** *"Crafted for unforgettable celebrations."*

## 2. Logo System & Rules

The brand identity system comprises three primary lockup configurations:

1. **Circular Atelier Crest (Primary Seal):**
   - Features the high-contrast monogram **`B`** adorned with a delicate botanical leaf counter, enclosed by architectural double rings and laurel branches in Dusty Brown and Rose Blonde.
   - Used on packaging box lids, menus, and hero brand moments.
2. **Horizontal Brand Lockup:**
   - Monogram emblem on the left paired with the serif wordmark `THE BEST HOME BAKER` and wide-tracked submark `BESPOKE ARTISANAL PÂTISSERIE`.
   - Used for website navigation headers, invoices, and digital communication.
3. **Monogram Seal:**
   - Single-color stamp for hot foil embossing on cake boxes and hand-stamped wax seals for silk packaging ribbons.

### 2.1 Logo Clearspace & Sizing Standards
- **Clearspace Rule:** The minimum clear space around any lockup must equal 50% of the crest height. No text, graphic elements, or borders may intrude within this perimeter.
- **Minimum Digital Sizes:**
  - Full Crest: 120px width minimum.
  - Horizontal Lockup: 160px width minimum.
  - Standalone Monogram: 32px height minimum (favicons, profile avatars).

## 5. Typography Hierarchy

### 5.1 Typeface Pairing
- **Display Serif:** `Cormorant Garamond` (Weights: 500, 600, 700). Evokes French culinary tradition, classical proportion, and artisanal luxury.
- **Body & Interface Sans:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Weights: 400, 500, 600). Clean geometric legibility, crisp digit rendering, effortless readability across mobile and desktop.
- **Micro Labels:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` medium uppercase with tracking `+0.04em` to `+0.08em` for tier specifications and portion details.

### 5.2 Typographic Scale

| Level | Font Family | Size (Desktop) | Line Height | Tracking | Weight |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Display H1** | Cormorant Garamond | 3.75rem (60px) | 1.06 | -0.02em | SemiBold (600) |
| **Section H2** | Cormorant Garamond | 2.75rem (44px) | 1.15 | -0.015em | SemiBold (600) |
| **Card H3** | Cormorant Garamond | 1.5rem (24px) | 1.25 | -0.01em | SemiBold (600) |
| **Body Large** | Plus Jakarta Sans | 1.125rem (18px) | 1.6 | Normal | Regular (400) |
| **Body Regular** | Plus Jakarta Sans | 1.0rem (16px) | 1.55 | Normal | Regular (400) |
| **Micro Labels** | Plus Jakarta Sans | 0.75rem (12px) | 1.2 | +0.08em | SemiBold (600) |

### 5.3 Typography Hard Rules
1. **Zero Em-Dash Rule:** Non-negotiable adherence to anti-slop guidelines. Em-dashes (` - `) and en-dashes (`-`) are strictly banned in copy, headings, buttons, and captions. Use regular hyphens with spaces (` - `), commas, or colons.
2. **Hero Copy Limits:** Hero headlines must fit within 2 lines. Hero subtext must not exceed 20 words.
3. **Eyebrow Restraint:** Maximum 1 eyebrow per 3 sections across any page.

## 6. Component System & States

- **Full Pill (`rounded-full` / 9999px):**
  - *Primary Button:* Espresso Noir `#231B16` background, Cotton Cream `#E4D8CB` label, tactile hover lift (`-translate-y-[1px]`), active push (`scale-[0.98]`).
  - *Secondary Button:* Transparent background with 1px `#A09086` Dusty Brown border and `#231B16` text.
  - *Badges:* Rose Blonde `#CFB3A9` background with `#231B16` text for high-contrast legibility.
- **Smooth Container (`rounded-2xl` / 16px):**
  - Used for portfolio cards, cake photography containers, pricing boxes, and modal panels.
  - 1px hairline border `#A09086` with Neutral Greige `#CDC6C3` fill.
- **Tactile Inputs (`rounded-xl` / 12px):**
  - Used for form fields, date selectors, number steppers, and textareas.
  - Cotton Cream `#E4D8CB` background with Dusty Brown `#A09086` border and focus aura.
