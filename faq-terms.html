Transport Instructions & Temperature Care

Always place the cake box on the flat floorboard of your vehicle (the passenger side footwell is best). Never place a cake on a slanted car seat or have a passenger hold it on their lap. The motion of the car and tilted angles will shift heavy tiers.

Turn your car's air conditioning on high at least 5 minutes prior to placing the cake inside. Drive gently, taking turns slowly and avoiding sudden braking.

Our cakes are made with real cultured butter, which sets firmly when cold. Keep your cake refrigerated until 1 to 2 hours before cutting (or 45 minutes in warm summer months). Serving at room temperature allows the buttercream to achieve its signature silky, melt-in-your-mouth texture.

Single-tier and 2-tier celebration cakes are packaged in reinforced bakery boxes suitable for client transport. For 3-tier and 4-tier wedding centerpieces, we strongly mandate our climate-controlled white-glove delivery service to guarantee safe transit and on-site assembly.

Deposit Terms & Payment Policies

A 50% non-refundable retainer is required to reserve your event date on our calendar. Because we accept only one or two bespoke commissions each weekend, your deposit reserves our kitchen and guarantees we decline other prospective events for that slot.

The final 50% balance and delivery fee are due exactly 14 calendar days prior to your celebration date. We accept bank transfers, major credit cards, and secure electronic invoices.

If you need to reschedule your event date, your deposit may be transferred to a future available date within 6 months, provided written notice is received at least 21 days prior to the original date. Cancellations made with less than 14 days notice are non-refundable.

Allergen Management & Dietary Accommodations

Please Note: While we follow strict sanitization and separation procedures, our home atelier kitchen regularly processes wheat, dairy, and eggs. We are not a certified sterile celiac facility.

We frequently use almond flour, Bronte pistachios, and roasted walnuts in specific flavor profiles. If an order specifies a nut allergy, we use isolated pans, dedicated mixing bowls, and fresh spatulas to prevent cross-contact. However, we cannot guarantee zero airborne traces.
