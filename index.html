Our Culinary Heritage

We are Devoted to Pure Craft

We believe celebrating life's most precious occasions calls for unhurried integrity. We churn real grass-fed cultured butter. We split single-origin Tahitian vanilla pods. We press edible pansies grown without pesticides. We bake every layer within 36 hours of celebration.

Notice Period

How far in advance should I book?

For wedding centerpieces, we recommend booking 3 to 6 months in advance. Celebration cakes require a minimum of 3 weeks notice.

Reserve Date
Safe Transit

How should I transport my cake?

Always place the cake box on the flat passenger footwell floor with AC turned on high. Never on slanted car seats or passenger laps.

Read Transport Guide →
Serving Temperature

When should the cake be served?

Real cultured butter sets firm in the fridge. Bring your cake to room temperature 1 to 2 hours before cutting for optimal silkiness.
